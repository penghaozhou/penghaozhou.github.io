About

I lead TikTok Live Content AI. Our team is responsible for live recommendation, multimodal content understanding, creator and viewer modeling, and AI-driven content governance.

We build models and systems for high-dynamic live scenarios: room and slice summaries, author personas, multimodal representations, content taxonomy, user-comment understanding, feed-quality models, and foundation models that support recommendation and moderation decisions.

Current Work

  • 01

    Live content representation

    Compact content codes for retrieval, ranking, and live-room understanding

    Building multimodal representations for live rooms, creators, and content slices, so recommendation systems can retrieve, rank, and reason over fast-changing live content more effectively.

  • 02

    LLM-driven recommendation

    Large models for user understanding, candidate retrieval, and ranking decisions

    Using LLMs to summarize long-term user interests, reason about live-room context, and improve both familiar recommendations and discovery in large-scale live feeds.

  • 03

    Feed quality and content safety AI

    Models and evaluation systems for healthier live feeds

    Leading model and system work that improves live feed quality and safety, including multimodal issue models, human-in-the-loop feedback, and automated evaluation workflows for policy-sensitive decisions.

  • 04

    Content labels and live understanding

    Structured signals from video, audio, language, comments, and creator interactions

    Turning live-room behavior, visual content, language, comments, and creator interaction signals into structured features that improve recommendation quality, safety decisions, and user experience.
