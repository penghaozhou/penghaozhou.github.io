Current Work

Live content changes moment by moment. My team brings together video, audio, language, and creator-viewer interactions to build models for recommendation, content understanding, and feed-quality governance. Our work spans four connected directions, supported by shared foundation models.

  • LLM-driven recommendation

    Modeling evolving user interests with LLMs to guide retrieval and ranking, balancing familiar preferences with the discovery of new creators and live content.

  • Feed-quality governance

    Combining multimodal issue detection, human feedback, and automated evaluation to improve content quality and safety decisions in live recommendation feeds.

  • Live content representation

    Learning multimodal embeddings and semantic codes for live rooms, creators, and content slices, so retrieval and ranking can generalize across short-lived, continuously changing content.

  • Live understanding

    Turning video, audio, language, and interactions into room and creator summaries, content categories, and contextual signals that make live experiences understandable to downstream models.

  • Live foundation models

    Developing large and compact multimodal models for live content, with a focus on audio and vision-language understanding through continued pretraining and task adaptation. We are building shared domain knowledge from explicit evaluation rubrics and model reasoning, while optimizing inference over densely sampled video frames. Together, these efforts provide a foundation for recommendation, representation, live understanding, and feed-quality governance, balancing model capability with serving efficiency.
